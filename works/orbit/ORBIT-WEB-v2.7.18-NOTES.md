# ORBIT Web v2.7.18 Notes

- Replaced the permanent close-range landing arc/end ticks with a transient RADAR ECHO.
- When a usable planet is within landing reveal range and ship speed is at or below landing speed, a brief scan return sweeps across the ship-facing limb.
- The echo is dark for most of its cycle and becomes slightly stronger/more frequent closer to the surface.
- Fast approaches show no landing echo and continue to behave as impacts/bounces.
- Automatic landing guide, landing logic, takeoff hold circle, MiniMap, SYSTEM log, ASTRA and RESTORE are unchanged.
