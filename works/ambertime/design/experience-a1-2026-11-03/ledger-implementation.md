## Final immediate-first-open matrix

`ledger-timing-table.md` has each fixture's first and repeat median and first maximum; `ledger-summary.json` adds means, phase distributions, and startup groups. Three fresh page loads per side per fixture, three consecutive opens of each view per load: two engines ×four fixtures ×three loads ×two sides ×three repeats ×two views =288 observations,144 before/after pairs. First-open figures have n=3; repeat figures n=6. Before/after order is paired, not randomized. There is no worker-ready gate or deliberate settling delay. Entry occurs as soon as the normal font-ready page and actual buttons can be used.

Representative first-complete-visible medians (ms): Chromium empty recipes128.7→62.7 and history128→60.7;120-month recipes465.8→340.5 and history250.4→162.8. WebKit empty recipes183→122 and history155→86;120-month recipes451→413 and history315→251. Legacy first medians also decrease in both engines. Repeat opens remain broadly similar, as expected from the existing snapshot cache.

**The improvement is not universal.** Chromium rich recipes median261.8→344.9ms (maximum329.4→411ms) is slower. WebKit long medians decrease but maxima worsen: recipes493→863ms and history397→685ms. Full replay remains and has not been changed or hidden. Three samples do not establish stable tails or statistical significance, and desktop WebKit does not establish physical iPhone Safari latency.

Aggregate startup medians across12 loads per side are Chromium app465.5→468ms and font480.2→488.85ms; WebKit app554.5→532.5ms and font581.5→559ms. These mixed changes do not establish startup acceleration or universal absence of regression. Scheduling preparation after the existing font-ready promise and first-paint opportunities prevents eager preparation before those points; it does not guarantee lower startup latency.

Fixtures use public operations and strict serializer/restore: empty; rich with3 archived tasting/bottling records;120-month current game with153 operations and1 archived record;12-month legacy progress through the existing adoption boundaries. This covers long elapsed time and operation history, not an extreme archive-size stress test. `ledger-fixture-provenance.json` records strict round-trip, construction, exact product-source hashes, and the final cleanup window. The portable runner reconstructs all fixtures; the earlier pre-delegation120-month simulation that could not lawfully migrate was excluded rather than edited into compatibility.

## Phase meaning and preservation

Browser capture records event timestamp→click listener, click→worker post/response, worker-handler compute, DOM mutation completion, and the following double RAF. The visible timestamp is an upper bound after one paint opportunity, not a hardware display timestamp. Content is fully present and an enabled button is checked by actual `elementFromPoint` at its center. Empty/loading content is not counted complete. Automation and scheduling contribute noise. Worker instrumentation adds diagnostic response metadata in served memory only; it does not alter files on disk, root behavior, saved state, or projection values.

All144 final before/after pairs have identical complete dialog HTML, HTML hash, focus, ARIA link, and scroll values. All48 actual worker-projection pairs (96 responses, request IDs/timing metadata excluded) have identical hashes. Every read-only open preserves exact localStorage raw bytes. Twelve rich-fixture probes at360×568 preserve nonzero recipe-list scroll through detail/back and return keyboard focus to the same recipe: actual clamp58px Chromium and62px WebKit. Five unit cases extract actual product functions and cover preparation-without-replay, unchanged-snapshot reuse, new-snapshot/out-of-order replies, closed-modal replies, errors/retry, and hidden startup.

Fresh page reloads recreate workers and receive their current persisted state; old-page replies cannot affect the new page. Root/formal regression remains responsible for changed-save operations, full replay, notification order, and historical baselines; this scoped runner does not replace it.
