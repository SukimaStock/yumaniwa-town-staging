ORBIT — Silent Reboot

Drift through a quiet universe in a small spacecraft, visiting planets to gather resources and data before returning HOME. Each return lets you reach a little farther the next time. ORBIT is a small exploration game built around the feeling that having a place to return to makes it possible to keep drifting.
